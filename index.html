    Sources & Methodology

    Where the numbers come from

    What each number means, where it comes from, and how it is counted.

    Definitions

    Planning Visits
    The number of planning visits for the months you picked.
    Clients Serviced During Planning Visits
    The number of clients the team served during planning visits in those months.
    Partners Visited
    A partner property visited in person during a planning visit. Counted since 2026, so there is no earlier year to compare with yet.
    Convention Groups Serviced
    The number of convention groups the team served.
    In-House Groups Serviced
    The number of in-house groups the team served. Counted since 2026, so there is no earlier year to compare with yet.
    Partner Referrals
    A referral that is not in person, such as an email, a phone call, or shared contact info. (Partners Visited is the in-person version.)

    Data sources

    Master.xlsx
    Every number on this dashboard comes from the Master.xlsx workbook in SharePoint (Business Intelligence-Apps site, DS&E folder). The BI team copies the latest numbers from it into the dashboard each month. The systems below feed that workbook.
    Granicus
    Planning visits, partner referrals and client services. Used on Team KPIs and Partner Referrals.
    Association Insights
    The client survey. Used on Client Survey.
    Internal Tracking
    The monthly Planning Visits sheet. Used on Overview and Team KPIs.

    Methodology

    Overview dates
    Start on January 1 of this year and end with the latest month that has numbers. Each number is compared with the same dates last year.
    When the newest month is not in yet
    If your dates go past the latest numbers, both years stop at the same month, so the comparison stays fair.
    The other tabs
    Each tab has its own filters. Changes compare the chosen year with the same months of the year before. Yearly survey ratings use full-year averages.
    Updates
    The monthly Planning Visits sheet in Master.xlsx is updated on the 15th of each month. The dashboard shows the date it was last refreshed at the top of the page.
